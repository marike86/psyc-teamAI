# Psychological Support and Effective Effort Team

[简体中文](README.md)

Invoke one team. Describe what happened, what you want to do, or what felt off about the last reply. One lead listens, delegates, repairs friction and resumes the task in the same conversation. The team includes 15 selectable psychological perspectives, four task specialists and an Emotional Companion.

## Examples in the same conversation

| You say | The team responds |
| --- | --- |
| I only want to talk about one thing today | The lead listens and stays with that issue |
| Compare attachment and mentalization | Call both members and distinguish what each notices |
| I feel discouraged, but I still want to finish this passage | Acknowledge the feeling and work through the passage together |
| I asked you to work alongside me. You made another plan | Own the specific mismatch and change the support, involving the Emotional Companion when useful |
| Let's talk about why this got to me, then go back to work | Pause and explore in the same chat, then pick up from the saved task position when asked |
| Keep this with the lead. Now help me do the first step | Respond without sharing the private content and begin the first step |
